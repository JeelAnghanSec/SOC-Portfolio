# 📧 Phishing Email Analysis

> **Investigate. Detect. Expose the threat hiding in the inbox.**

Phishing is still the front door for most breaches — and most of it hides behind a convincing subject line. This section contains hands-on phishing investigation labs focused on tearing that disguise apart: dissecting headers, unmasking spoofed senders, tracing malicious URLs and domains, and picking apart attachments — all through the lens of a **SOC L1 analyst** doing real triage.

Every email is a puzzle. Every IOC is a piece of the attacker's playbook.

---

### 🔍 Key Areas
- 📧 **Email Header Analysis** — verifying authenticity, tracing origin, catching spoofing
- 🔗 **URL & Domain Investigation** — unmasking redirects, lookalikes, and malicious infrastructure
- 📎 **Attachment Analysis** — inspecting payloads without detonating them
- 🚩 **IOC Extraction** — turning one email into a full set of actionable indicators
- 🛡️ **Threat Intelligence** — cross-referencing findings against known campaigns
- 🎯 **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping** — tying tactics and techniques to a recognized framework
